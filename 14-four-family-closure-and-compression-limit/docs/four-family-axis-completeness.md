# Four-Family Axis Completeness (Ψ = (𝒮, 𝒢, 𝒞, 𝒪))

**Status: reported from a separate conversational record, not independently
re-verified with frozen protocol/fixture/harness files in this archive
edition.** No `scripts/` or `results/*.json` back this document; it is a
narrative reconstruction of an earlier campaign, included because the
manuscript relies on its conclusion (the localization premise used by the
Compression Limit Theorem, `compression-limit-theorem.md`). Readers who need
executable evidence should treat this document as a hypothesis statement, not
as a verified result of this archive.

## 1. Context: why an axis-completeness question at all

The manuscript's bridge hypothesis `H_CO⁺` needs residual variation in
functional access A* to be attributable to active causal organization 𝒞
specifically — not to knowledge (I_G / 𝒢), embodiment or situatedness (𝒮),
or some undeclared further primitive. This document summarizes the argument
that a four-family ontology

```
Ψ(e) = (𝒮, 𝒢, 𝒞, 𝒪)
```

is complete for agent–environment functional properties, with no fifth
primitive family and no sixth.

`𝒪` ("functional orientation") bundles agency/control, valence, self/world
boundary, and temporal horizon. It is demonstrably **not** part of 𝒞 and
plays no role in the C_O / A* / A†_I bridge developed in the rest of this
manuscript; it is recorded here only to close the ontology.

## 2. Steps of the argument, as reconstructed

1. **Three axes are at least partially distinct.** Comparing modern human,
   prehistoric human, and infant profiles shows that I_G (knowledge), S
   (situatedness/embodiment), and 𝒞 (causal-organizational competence) vary
   at least partially independently of one another — no pair collapses onto
   the other across the three cases.

2. **Cognitive-functional properties sit on the same three axes.** At fixed
   evaluation protocol, the standard list of functional-cognitive properties
   (attention, working memory, planning, world-model / causal representation
   of the environment, creativity, learning, memory-as-reconstruction-and-use
   of the past — remembering is not merely possessing a memory trace —,
   abstraction/compositionality, causal and counterfactual reasoning) each
   decompose onto (I_G, S, 𝒞) without requiring a further axis.

3. **The third family is necessarily organizational.** Whatever is left over
   once I_G and S are fixed, and which nonetheless explains variation in
   these functional-cognitive properties, is by construction not a knowledge
   fact and not a situational fact — it is a fact about how the system's
   parts are causally organized. This is the pre-theoretic slot that 𝒞 (and
   later C_O) formalizes.

4. **(I_G, S, 𝒞) is not complete for agent–environment properties.** A
   distinct family of properties — agency/control, valence, self/world
   boundary, temporal horizon — is not reducible to any combination of
   I_G, S, 𝒞. This is the AXIS-4 (𝒪) family.

5. **Ψ(e) = (𝒮, 𝒢, 𝒞, 𝒪) is complete.** A "no fifth family" campaign tested
   and absorbed six further candidate axes — plasticity, meta-plasticity,
   resource cost, causal latency, cross-family binding, lesion robustness —
   through a witness/repair cycle (each candidate was either shown to
   decompose onto an existing family, or shown not to be a primitive
   agent-environment property in its own right). The campaign's own stated
   conclusion was:

   > **FOUR-FAMILY ONTOLOGY — STRONG FINITE NON-REFUTATION**

   explicitly caveated by its authors as a *non-refutation* result (no
   counterexample was found across the tested candidates), not a metaphysical
   completeness proof. No sixth family was found or proposed.

## 3. What this licenses, and what it does not

**Licensed:** treating (𝒮, 𝒢, 𝒞, 𝒪) as the working ontology for
agent–environment functional properties, and treating 𝒞 as the family that
must carry any residual variation in A* once I_G, S, and O-type properties
are held fixed or ruled irrelevant to the question at hand (§ manuscript
argument for `H_CO⁺`).

**Not licensed:** any claim that this closure, by itself, shows that the
*compact declared profile* `C_O = [(Γ, Δ, R(k), Λ)]` — as opposed to 𝒞 in
full — captures all of that residual variation. That is a separate question,
addressed by the Compression Limit Theorem (`compression-limit-theorem.md`)
and tested empirically by the organizational-closure repair chain in folder
`15` (`docs/organizational-closure-repair-chain.md`).
