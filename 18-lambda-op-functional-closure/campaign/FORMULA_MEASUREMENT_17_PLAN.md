# Qwen3-8B Formula Measurement 17 Plan

Status: implemented and ready to run under the frozen Formula Contract 16.

The implementation is specified by `FORMULA_MEASUREMENT_17_PROTOCOL.md`. It measures finite-battery Gamma, Delta, an adapted Qwen re-entry R, and a finite joint typed Lambda signature at fixed OP level on 12 development systems. Raw measurement is target-blind. Target-facing evaluation is deferred until the panel is complete and is limited to 11 preregistered component families at fixed resolution.

No transition to ROLE, PARAM_OP, ALGEBRA, or ORBIT_QUOTIENT is authorized by this phase. A development candidate, if found, must be frozen and tested once on a fresh holdout with no refit.
