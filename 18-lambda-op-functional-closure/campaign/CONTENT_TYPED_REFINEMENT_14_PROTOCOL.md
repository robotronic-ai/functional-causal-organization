# Content-Typed Pooled160 Refinement 14 Protocol

## Goal

Search quickly for a compact typed content-effect Lambda quotient after Fresh Validation 13 showed that the three-feature quotient transported in fidelity but remained functionally non-closed.

This phase does not introduce a fifth organizational component. It enriches the existing operation-indexed Lambda proxy.

## Opened data

The search uses three opened panels as exploratory training data:

- Construction 12: 32 systems, SHA-256 `9b2b9ec96836d39383852ac666ddc2c4aa362473ebed7e9b52d24270e6a8d90a`
- Fresh Validation 12 after refutation: 64 systems, SHA-256 `09b7c65d30754f999fe1d93ed53ca2df4eba982ae1ac4c4ba3f740a86c8f4d40`
- Fresh Validation 13 after refutation: 64 systems, SHA-256 `92d9a79472c05cf091f823ed5e970542926106bbf13dd7e1ce16737fa5e0dff4`

Total opened systems: 160.

## Measurement space

The rich exploratory proxy remains the fixed 24D signed content-effect kernel over:

- operations: `REVOKE`, `SUBSTITUTE`
- addresses: `H1`, `H2`, `H3`
- experimental interventions: `ZERO_SUFFIX`, `HEAD_ROTATE`
- margins: `DONOR_MINUS_ORIGINAL`, `NONE_MINUS_ORIGINAL`

The descriptor does not use baseline outcomes or baseline absolute log-probabilities.

## Search

Standardization and quantile edges are fit on the 160 opened systems without behavioral targets. Feature selection is explicitly target-informed.

The first search expands the three coordinates found by Refinement 13:

- `REVOKE:H2:HEAD_ROTATE:DONOR_MINUS_ORIGINAL`, 8 bins
- `SUBSTITUTE:H3:HEAD_ROTATE:DONOR_MINUS_ORIGINAL`, 3 bins
- `SUBSTITUTE:H3:HEAD_ROTATE:NONE_MINUS_ORIGINAL`, 8 bins

Additional coordinates may be added up to a total of eight. If seed expansion finds no useful state, the engine searches `SUBSTITUTE` globally and then the complete `REVOKE+SUBSTITUTE` feature family.

## Useful exploratory candidate rule

A candidate must satisfy all of the following on the pooled 160 systems:

- zero functional collision pairs
- nontrivial coverage at least 20%
- within-fiber distortion at most 25%
- nontrivial coverage at least 20% on each of the three opened panels

Any selected candidate is exploratory because feature selection uses the behavioral target.

## Fresh Validation 14

A selected candidate is copied unchanged before schedule construction. Fresh Validation 14 uses 64 new systems and excludes exact binding/donor tuples from all available opened panels, including Fresh Validation 13.

No standardizer, feature, bin count, or quantile edge may be refit on Fresh Validation 14.
