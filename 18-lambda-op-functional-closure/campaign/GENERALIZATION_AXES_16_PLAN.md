The campaign no longer treats ID, OP, ROLE, PARAM_OP, ALGEBRA, and ORBIT_QUOTIENT as one monotone ladder. The search object is:

`C_O^(A,Q,rho)`

where:

- `A` is the declared intervention algebra. Moving from ID to OP, PARAM_OP, or a generated ALGEBRA generally adds causal distinctions and can make the induced partition finer.

Algebra richness and quotient strength are distinct axes. ALGEBRA is not automatically a stronger generalizer than OP; its primary value is transport under composition and temporal closure.

## Prospective selection criterion

Selection is performed on real Qwen development systems, not on synthetic witnesses. Synthetic systems serve as mathematical and implementation tests only.

For each preregistered candidate partition, report:

1. heterogeneous collision count and normalized sufficiency loss `H(A_dagger | C) / H(A_dagger)`;
2. adjusted mutual information (AMI) to the complete behavioral-profile partition;
3. a deterministic permutation control that preserves descriptor fiber sizes;
4. descriptor partition cost: fiber count, fiber fraction, and partition entropy;
5. nontrivial coverage.

A candidate is screen-admissible only if its residual functional loss is compatible with the measured behavioral noise floor and its AMI exceeds the 95th percentile of the fixed-fiber permutation control. Among admissible candidates, prefer the coarsest partition.

AMI is evaluation-only. It must not be used to construct component coordinates, roles, symmetries, macro variables, or resolution.
