# Compression Limit for CF-to-A Sufficiency v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-14

## Proposition

Let:

\[
p:\mathcal C\rightarrow Z
\]

be any organizational profile.

Suppose \(p\) is non-injective, so there exist:

\[
\mathcal C_1\neq\mathcal C_2
\]

with:

\[
p(\mathcal C_1)=p(\mathcal C_2).
\]

If the admissible target class contains a functional property \(A^\star\) that
distinguishes those two organizations, then no function \(H\) can satisfy:

\[
A^\star=H\circ p.
\]

### Proof

Assume \(A^\star=H\circ p\).  Then:

\[
p(\mathcal C_1)=p(\mathcal C_2)
\]

implies:

\[
H(p(\mathcal C_1))=H(p(\mathcal C_2)),
\]

hence:

\[
A^\star(\mathcal C_1)=A^\star(\mathcal C_2),
\]

contradicting the hypothesis.

QED.

## Corollary for the manuscript

Four-family closure:

\[
\Psi=(\mathcal S,\mathcal G,\mathcal C,\mathcal O)
\]

can localize a residual functional variation in \(\mathcal C\).

It cannot, by itself, prove that a non-injective compression:

\[
C_F=p(\mathcal C)
\]

is sufficient for functional access.

A separate restriction on the target construct \(A\), a representation theorem,
or empirical evidence is necessary.

## Why the recent counterexamples matter

The typed-binding and joint-synergy witnesses are concrete examples of
organizations that are distinguished by access consequences but were collapsed
by progressively richer compressions.

They instantiate the abstract proposition rather than merely exposing coding
bugs.

## Limit of the repair strategy

One can always retain more of the intervention-response structure.

In the limit, a complete typed interventional causal model can become
sufficient for every functional consequence defined on that model.

But then the explanatory profile has approached \(\mathcal C\) itself.

Therefore the scientific objective is not:

> keep enriching \(C_F\) until it is trivially sufficient for every possible
> functional consequence.

The useful target is:

> identify a compact, target-independent causal profile that prospectively
> predicts a preregistered family of access consequences better than rival
> variables.

That is construct validation, not taxonomic closure.
