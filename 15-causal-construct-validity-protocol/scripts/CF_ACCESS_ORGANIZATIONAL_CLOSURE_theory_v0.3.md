## 2. Four-family localization theorem — conditional form

Let the declared functional-agent ontology be:

\[
\Psi
=
(\mathcal S,\mathcal G,\mathcal C,\mathcal O)
\]

with its reachability-closed intervention structure.

Assume:

1. \(A\) is reachability-admissible in the declared domain;
2. the four-family reachability closure applies;
3. at fixed \(\mathcal C\), \(A\) is invariant to admissible changes in
   \(\mathcal S,\mathcal G,\mathcal O\).

Then there exists \(H_C\) such that:

\[
\boxed{
A
=
H_C(\mathcal C).
}
\]

### Proof

Reachability closure gives:

\[
A
=
F_A(\mathcal S,\mathcal G,\mathcal C,\mathcal O).
\]

By assumption 3, \(F_A\) is constant on every fiber obtained by changing
\(\mathcal S,\mathcal G,\mathcal O\) at fixed \(\mathcal C\).  Therefore
\(F_A\) descends to a well-defined function of \(\mathcal C\) alone.

QED.

### Scientific status

The theorem is exact conditional on its assumptions.

FCI-1 supplies finite synthetic support for assumption 3; it does not prove
global invariance.
