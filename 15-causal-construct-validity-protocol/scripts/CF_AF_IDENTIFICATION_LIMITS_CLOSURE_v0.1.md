The AF battery remains useful as an independent consequence battery.

It is not a structural identification test.

\[
\mathcal A_F>0\Rightarrow \Gamma>0,\Delta_{\rm prop}>0,R>0

does not survive.

This does not establish anything negative about latent functional access
consciousness \(A\).  It establishes an identification limit of finite
behavioral indicators.

Any finite behavioral input/output law can be implemented by an acyclic
finite-horizon causal network with fresh time-indexed computation.

Therefore finite extensional behavior cannot, by itself, prove recurrence.

The relation between \(C_F\) and \(A\) must be tested through prospective
causal manipulation and discriminant prediction, not inferred from AF behavior
alone.
